# Clock Rings — Support

A dual decimal + standard clock and timecard for shift and postal workers.

**Need help?** Email [Eric@EricRogersTechnology.com](mailto:Eric@EricRogersTechnology.com)
and I'll get back to you. Including your device and iOS version helps me answer faster.

For TestFlight beta testers: you can also send feedback directly through the TestFlight
app — screenshot → tap **Send Beta Feedback**.

## Frequently asked

**How do I track a shift?**
On the Clock screen, tap *Begin Tour* to clock in, then use *Out / In to Lunch*, breaks,
and *End Tour* as your day goes. Forgot to clock in? Use *Begin Tour at Custom Time* to
backfill the right start time — even an earlier date for an overnight tour.

**How does Pay Estimate work?**
In Settings → Pay Estimate, you enter *your own* hourly rate and any overtime / premium
rates — the app never assumes a number. Reports then shows an estimated gross from the
hours you've tracked. It's an estimate and excludes taxes and deductions; it may differ
from your official pay.

**Does my data sync across devices?**
Yes — punches, schedules, alarms, and your active tour sync across iPhone, iPad, and
Apple Watch through *your own* iCloud. Make sure each device is signed into the same
Apple Account with iCloud Drive on.

**Where is my data stored? Is it private?**
Everything stays on your device and in your private iCloud. There's no account, no
server, no ads, and no tracking — the developer never sees your data. See the
[Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.html) for details.

**How do I add a widget?**
Touch and hold the Home Screen → tap **+** (top-left) → search "Clock Rings" → pick a
widget and add it. If a widget ever looks blank or out of date, remove it and re-add it.

**The widget isn't following Light/Dark — why?**
Home-Screen widgets follow the app's Appearance setting per device (Settings → Clock &
Appearance → Appearance). After changing it, remove and re-add the widget so iOS picks
up the change.

**What's the USPS Virtual Timecard button?**
A convenience link that opens the public USPS web timecard in Safari. Clock Rings is
independent and is **not affiliated with USPS** — it never logs in or reads anything
from that page. "Reconcile with Virtual Timecard" only compares hours you paste in, on
your device.

**How do I leave a tip?**
Settings → About & Support → Tip Jar. Tips are optional, one-time, and unlock nothing —
just a thank-you that supports development.
